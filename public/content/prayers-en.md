## Our Father
<!-- id: our-father -->
**Category:** Traditional
**Duration:** 1 min

### Description
The prayer Jesus taught his disciples.

### Prayer
Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come, thy will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread, and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

### Latin
Pater noster, qui es in cælis: sanctificetur nomen tuum; adveniat regnum tuum; fiat voluntas tua, sicut in cælo et in terra. Panem nostrum quotidianum da nobis hodie; et dimitte nobis debita nostra, sicut et nos dimittimus debitoribus nostris; et ne nos inducas in tentationem; sed libera nos a malo. Amen.

## Hail Mary
<!-- id: hail-mary -->
**Category:** Marian
**Duration:** 1 min

### Description
A prayer asking Mary to pray with us and for us.

### Prayer
Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

### Latin
Ave Maria, gratia plena, Dominus tecum. Benedicta tu in mulieribus, et benedictus fructus ventris tui, Iesus. Sancta Maria, Mater Dei, ora pro nobis peccatoribus, nunc et in hora mortis nostrae. Amen.

## Glory Be
<!-- id: glory-be -->
**Category:** Traditional
**Duration:** 1 min

### Description
A short doxology of praise to the Holy Trinity.

### Prayer
Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.

### Latin
Gloria Patri, et Filio, et Spiritui Sancto. Sicut erat in principio, et nunc, et semper, et in sæcula sæculorum. Amen.

## Act of Contrition
<!-- id: act-of-contrition -->
**Category:** Sacraments
**Duration:** 2 min

### Description
A prayer of sorrow and a renewed desire to follow Christ.

### Prayer
My God, I am sorry for my sins with all my heart. In choosing to do wrong and failing to do good, I have sinned against you, whom I should love above all things. I firmly intend, with your help, to do penance, to sin no more, and to avoid whatever leads me to sin. Amen.

## Morning Offering
<!-- id: morning-offering -->
**Category:** Daily
**Duration:** 2 min

### Description
Begin the day by offering its joys and challenges to God.

### Prayer
God of this new morning, I offer you the thoughts, words, work, and encounters of this day. Bless what is good, guide what is uncertain, and help me meet each person with patience and love. May all I do draw me closer to you. Amen.

## Prayer for Peace
<!-- id: prayer-for-peace -->
**Category:** Hope & Peace
**Duration:** 2 min

### Description
A moment to pray for peace in our hearts and in the world.

### Prayer
Prince of Peace, quiet what is restless within me. Give courage to those who work for reconciliation, comfort to those living through conflict, and wisdom to those entrusted with difficult choices. Make me a patient listener and a small instrument of your peace. Amen.

## Evening Gratitude
<!-- id: evening-gratitude -->
**Category:** Daily
**Duration:** 2 min

### Description
End the day by noticing its gifts and placing tomorrow in God's care.

### Prayer
Gracious God, thank you for the gifts and graces of this day, both seen and unseen. Receive the work left unfinished and the worries I carry. Forgive my failures, bless those I love, and grant us peaceful rest beneath your loving care. Amen.

## Prayer for the Sick
<!-- id: prayer-for-the-sick -->
**Category:** Hope & Peace
**Duration:** 2 min

### Description
Entrust those who are ill to the healing compassion of Christ.

### Prayer
Jesus, companion of the suffering, draw near to all who are sick and those who care for them. Bring relief where there is pain, strength where there is weariness, and hope where the way feels long. Help me serve with tenderness and trust in your mercy. Amen.
